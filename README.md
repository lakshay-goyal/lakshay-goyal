# Hey, I'm Lakshay Goyal 👋

### A bit about me

I'm just a developer who loves building random shit, experimenting with everything, breaking things, fixing them (sometimes), and learning a ton along the way.

I have this terrible habit of wanting to become a **polymath**. From code, AI, and design to philosophy, psychology, politics, and every other weird little thing life has to offer. But don't mistake me for a generalist. I love going **deep down rabbit holes**, sometimes so deep I forget why I jumped in to begin with.

Over the years, I've messed around with **mobile, web, AI engineering, DevOps**, and pretty much anything that caught my attention. Built things end-to-end, broke a bunch of stuff, figured things out, and collected more lessons than I can count.

But somewhere along the way, I realized something: **building cool shit is fun, but building things that genuinely make someone's life better? That's a whole different kind of fun.**

So now, I'm trying to crawl out of the development rabbit hole and put everything I've learned into something more practical. Building products, solving actual problems, and helping people and businesses work smarter, waste less time, and focus on the things that matter.

Because at the end of the day, technology should make life easier, not become another reason to forget to live it.

And hey, maybe it'll help someone **touch some grass, spend more time with their kids, and enjoy being human for a change.**
